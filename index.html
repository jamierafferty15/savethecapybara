About the game

A big storm has blown the capybaras up into the clouds, and they are floating down on leaf parachutes. Your job is to catch every one on your lily pad and bring them home to the warm hot spring.

How to play

  • Move your lily pad by dragging your finger, moving the mouse or using the arrow keys.
  • Catch the capybaras before they splash into the river.
  • Keep away from spiky conkers. They cost you a heart.
  • Catch an orange to get a heart back. Golden capybaras count as two.
  • Catch 5 in a row for bonus points.

Worlds and friends

There are 18 levels across six worlds: Sunny Skies, Windy Day, Starry Night, Autumn Leaves, Holiday Snow and Space Splash. Every level you finish brings a new friend to your Capy Club. Finish a level without losing a heart to earn all 3 stars.

Easy, Medium or Hard

Easy gives you 5 hearts and slower capybaras. Hard is super speedy, for bigger kids and grown-ups. Each one keeps its own stars.

For grown-ups
